Wideline Glass Partition

Professional project · Saint-Gobain

A customisable, modular aluminium and glass partition system. The Wideline Clipper supports glass up to 17.52 mm thick while delivering sound insulation of up to 52 dB.

Profiles snap-fit together and are secured with self-drill screws, so the system is quick to assemble and easy to configure for different offices.

03

Dual-Axis Bearing

Granted patent · Saint-Gobain · Norglide Bearings

A bearing assembly for solar-tracking structures. A fixed housing is attached to the support beam, and an adjustable housing carries the rail with a low-friction interface between the two.

The adjustable housing self-aligns, so the rail can sit slightly out of line with the beam's central axis without binding.
